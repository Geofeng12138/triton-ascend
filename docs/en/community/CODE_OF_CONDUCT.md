# Contributor Covenant

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community a place that is friendly, safe, and fair for everyone.

We pledge to foster an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of every individual, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical appearance, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religious belief, nationality or social origin, socioeconomic status, education, or other identity attributes. All who sincerely participate and abide by this Covenant have an equal right to participate.

## Encouraged Behaviors

While social norms may vary, we all strive to meet the community's expectations for positive behavior. We also understand that, due to differences in culture, background, or native language, how others interpret our words and actions may differ from our intentions.

With these considerations in mind, we commit to treating each other with care and to adhering to the following shared values as guiding principles:

1. Respect the **purpose of our community**, its activities, and ways of gathering.
2. Interact with others with **goodwill and honesty**.
3. Respect **different perspectives and experiences**.
4. Take **responsibility** for one's own words, actions, and contributions.
5. Give and receive **constructive feedback** in an appropriate manner.
6. Commit to **making amends** when harm is caused.
7. Engage in other behaviors that benefit the **well-being of the community**.

## Restricted Behaviors

We agree to restrict the following behaviors within the community. Engaging in these behaviors, threatening to engage in them, or promoting them constitutes a violation of this Code of Conduct.

1. **Harassment**: Violating boundaries after they have been clearly expressed, or continuing unwanted personal attention after being explicitly asked to stop.
2. **Personal attacks**: Making insulting, degrading, or contemptuous remarks directed at community members or groups.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination**: Judging others' character or behavior based on immutable identities or characteristics.
4. **Sexualization**: Engaging in intimate actions that are generally considered inappropriate within the context or purpose of the community.
5. **Breach of confidentiality**: Sharing or using others' personal or private information without permission.
6. **Harmful behavior**: Committing, inciting, or threatening violence or other harm against any person or group.
7. Other behaviors that threaten the **well-being of the community**.

### Additional Restricted Behaviors

1. **False identity**: Impersonating others for any reason, or pretending to be someone else to evade regulatory measures.
2. **Improper attribution**: Failing to properly attribute the source of contributed content.
3. **Promotional material**: Sharing marketing or other commercial content in ways that do not align with community norms.
4. **Improper dissemination**: Failing to responsibly present content that includes, links to, or describes any other restricted behaviors.

## Reporting Issues

All reports of Code of Conduct violations will be investigated, which may include reviewing messages, logs, and recordings, or interviewing witnesses and other relevant participants. Community administrators will prioritize safety and confidentiality while maintaining transparency in the investigation and enforcement process as much as possible. To uphold these values, enforcement measures will be conducted in private settings involving the parties concerned; however, with the consent of all parties, notifying the entire community about the incident may also be part of the resolution.

## Handling and Remedying Harm

**[Note: The handling measures and remedies listed below are suggestions based on best practices in Code of Conduct enforcement. If your community already has an established enforcement process, please ensure this section is modified to describe your own policies.]**

If, after investigation, community administrators confirm a violation of this Code of Conduct, they will refer to the following "graduated response" mechanism to determine the most appropriate remedy based on the impact of the incident on the individuals involved and the community as a whole. Depending on the severity of the violation, lower-level measures may be bypassed.

1) **Warning**
   1) **Incident**: A single or continuous violation.
   2) **Consequence**: A private written warning from the community administrator.
   3) **Remedy**: Remediation may include a written private apology, acknowledging one's responsibility, or actively clarifying how to meet expectations in the future.

2) **Temporary Restriction**
   1) **Incident**: Repeated violations that have been previously warned about, or a first moderately serious violation.
   2) **Consequence**: A private written warning accompanied by a time-limited cooling-off period, intended to emphasize the seriousness of the situation and allow the involved community member time to process the incident. The cooling-off period may involve restrictions on specific communication channels or interactions with specific community members.
   3) **Remedy**: Remediation may include an apology, using the cooling-off period to reflect on one's behavior and its impact, and fully understanding how to re-enter the community space after the period ends.

3) **Temporary Suspension**
   1) **Incident**: A pattern of repeated violations after multiple warnings from the community administrator, or a single serious violation.
   2) **Consequence**: A private written warning with conditions required for reinstatement. Typically, temporary suspension aims to give the suspended individual time to reflect on their behavior and consider possible corrective actions.
   3) **Remedy**: Conditions for remediation include respecting the intent of the suspension, meeting the specified conditions for reinstatement, and fully understanding how to reintegrate into the community after the suspension is lifted.

4) **Permanent Ban**
   1) **Incident**: Multiple violations of the Code of Conduct where other graduated measures have failed to resolve the issue, or a violation so severe that the community administrator determines the community cannot be safe with the individual remaining.
   2) **Consequence**: Revocation of access to all community spaces, tools, and communication channels. Generally, permanent bans should be used rarely, must have sufficient and compelling reasons, and should only be implemented as a last resort when other remedies have failed to change behavior.
   3) **Remedy**: In such severe cases, no feasible remedy exists.

This graduated response mechanism is intended as a guideline and does not limit community administrators from exercising their discretion and judgment in the best interest of the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces or other settings. Representing the community includes, but is not limited to: using an official email address; posting via an official social media account; or acting as a designated representative at online or offline events.
